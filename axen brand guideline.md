01. Introduction

Welcome to the AXEN brand guidelines.
This document defines our visual and verbal identity.

Welcome!

This brand identity style guide is your resource for understanding and communicating the AXEN brand consistently.

Inside you will find our story, our verbal and visual identity standards, and guidelines for bringing our brand to life across all touchpoints.

Consistency builds trust. Every interaction with our brand should feel unmistakably AXEN.

02. Our Story

Our Mission, Our Vision, and the story behind AXEN.

AXEN was founded on a simple belief: that cognitive wellness should be accessible, natural, and dignified.

We create science-backed supplements for adults who want to nurture their mental clarity without compromising their values.

Our Mission

To reclaim your peace of mind by turning cognitive wellness into a calm and trusted ritual, rooted in science and transparency.

Our Vision

A world where aging gracefully includes a clear, confident mind, supported by products people can truly trust.

06. The Wordmark & Icon

Logo, clearspace, and usage guidelines.

The Wordmark

The AXEN wordmark is the primary brand identifier.
It should be used consistently across all applications.

Variants:

Base

Secondary 2

Secondary 1

Highlight

Primary

The Icon

The neuron icon symbolizes cognitive wellness and neural connection.
It can be used independently or paired with the wordmark.

Wordmark + Icon

Combined lockups for flexible brand application across backgrounds and formats.

The Logo

Full logo versions combining wordmark and icon across all color variants.

Clearspace

Maintain minimum clearspace around the logo equal to the height of the “X” in AXEN.

This ensures visibility and impact.

Logo Misuses

Protect the integrity of our brand by avoiding these common mistakes:

Don’t stretch or distort

Don’t rotate the logo

Don’t add effects or shadows

Don’t change the colors

Don’t use low contrast

Don’t add outlines

08. Typography

Primary and secondary typefaces.

Primary Typeface

Nexa

Nexa and its variations are our primary typeface.
Its clean, humanist forms ensure maximum legibility for our 50+ demographic while maintaining professional sophistication.

Type Hierarchy

H1 — Headlines (Black)

H2 — Subheadings (Extra Bold)

H3 — Section titles (Normal)

Body — Paragraph text (Light)

Caption — Supporting text / metadata (Extra Light)
